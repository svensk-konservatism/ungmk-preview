About us

Ungdom mot kommunism (UMK – Youth Against Communism) is a youth organisation working against communism's influence in society, in the fight for a better Sweden.

Who we are

UMK is a non-profit association, organisation number 802554-4308. Membership is free if you're under 26.

What we do

Ungdom mot kommunism is an anti-communist resistance movement for young people. We see communism as one of the greatest threats to a free and democratic society, and we exist to fight it.

Our core is at universities and colleges. There, our members shape opinion in local campus groups and challenge communist ideas among students.

We stand on conservative values, are independent of all parties and reject every movement that works against the fight against communism.

  1. School and campus events

    We come to schools and campuses and talk about what communist regimes have actually done, from the Soviet Union and Mao's China to Cambodia, North Korea and Cuba.

  2. Themed evenings

    One topic per evening, from the Gulag to today's Cuba, with sources, quotes and documents – not rumours.

  3. Training sessions

    We learn the history and the arguments together, so you can stand up for freedom in the classroom, on campus and in society.

  4. Campaigns

    We campaign against communism in Sweden and bring together young people across the country who share our convictions.
