Kommunismens återkomst i väst

The ideas, the violence and freedom in our time

Communism is again being presented as a promise, revolutionary party programmes live on, and parts of the radical left have significant support among young people. At the same time, communist party-states continue to restrict people's freedom. This report examines these developments and the political crime found in some radical-left milieus, as well as Sweden's relationship with democratic Taiwan. 33 pages.

The report is in Swedish.
