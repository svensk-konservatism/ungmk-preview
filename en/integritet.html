Privacy policy

This page explains what personal data is processed when you become a member of Ungdom mot kommunism, why it happens and what you can decide for yourself.

1.Data controller

The data controller is the association Ungdom mot kommunism (UMK), a non-profit association with organisation number 802554-4308. Send any questions about your data to us – see section 11.

2.Data we process

Membership is free if you're under 26. You apply for membership through Registra's form, which is embedded on the site. The form asks for first name, surname, Swedish personal identity number, gender, email address and phone number.

The personal identity number is needed so that each member can be identified unambiguously and so that the association can verify its membership numbers when applying for state or organisational grants, for example from the Swedish Agency for Youth and Civil Society (MUCF). The personal identity number is not used for any other purpose.

In addition to the above, the association may record your address, municipality and the date your membership began. We do not collect more data than membership requires.

3.Why the data is processed

The data is used only to register your membership and to contact you about the association's activities.

Being a member reveals a political opinion. That is why the data is handled with extra care.

4.Legal basis

The data is processed to fulfil your membership, which is an agreement between you and the association (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation). Data that forms part of the association's accounts is processed to comply with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)), and certain processing, such as information about the association's activities, is based on the association's legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)). Because membership reveals a political opinion, this data is processed under Article 9(2)(d), which allows a non-profit body with a political aim to process data about its members. The data is not disclosed outside the association without your consent.

5.Who can access the data

The membership register is managed by Registra, which stores the data on the association's behalf as a data processor. Beyond that, the data is not shared with anyone outside the association.

Within the association, only the board and authorised officers have access to the membership register, and only to the extent they need it for their role. Suppliers that handle data on the association's behalf, for example for the membership register and email, do so as data processors under data processing agreements and may not use the data for their own purposes.

The data is processed primarily within the EU/EEA. If it is ever transferred to a country outside the EU/EEA, this happens only with safeguards under Chapter V of the GDPR, for example the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6.How long the data is kept

The data is kept for as long as you are a member.

When your membership ends, the data is deleted within a reasonable time, no later than two years after your membership ended. Data that forms part of accounting records is kept for as long as the Swedish Bookkeeping Act requires, currently seven years.

7.Cookies and statistics

This website uses no cookies. The membership form is loaded from Registra, which may use its own cookies under its own terms.

8.Security

Access to the register is limited to authorised people and controlled through personal permissions. Logins are protected by passwords and, where possible, two-factor authentication, and data is transferred over encrypted connections.

9.Your rights

You have the right to:

  • find out what data we hold about you and get a copy of it,
  • have incorrect data corrected,
  • have your data deleted,
  • request that processing is restricted, or object to it,
  • withdraw any consent you have given. This does not affect processing that has already taken place.

Email info@ungmk.se to exercise your rights.

10.Complaints

If you believe your data is being handled incorrectly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

11.Contact and changes

Send questions about privacy and your data to info@ungmk.se.

When the processing changes, we update this page. The date below shows when it was last changed.
